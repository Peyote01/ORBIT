Read the flight manual

Press C to accept a free contract anywhere. Hold Shift to move twice as fast while stamina lasts. Captain chooses coordinates at navigation. Grapple and rotate the wreck at the scanner; V scans a component. Identification is optional. Compare clues to ship posters to plan your salvage.

For bulky salvage, walk aft and press H near the rear cargo hatch to open both doors. Bring cargo through the wide opening, then close it before departure. The starboard side airlock remains available for EVA. Press T to equip the handheld tractor (equipped by default); aim and left-click to attach loose cargo, use the mouse wheel to adjust distance, hold R and move the mouse to rotate, then E on a green cargo grid to secure. Right-click or Q releases. E can still pick up loose crates using the old method. Crunch destroys everything still inside the wreck and produces Scrap. Sell at either market; compare prices before traveling.

First departure each quota is free. Further station departures and leaving a wreck after taking salvage consume one day. Pay the quota after seven days. One extension per ship adds seven days to the same quota. Failure to pay repossesses everything and resets credits to 1,000.
